From drawing to prediction

  1. Your pen strokes are cropped to their bounding box and centered, so the digit's size and position don't matter.
  2. They are redrawn at a fixed pen thickness relative to the digit, then averaged down to an 8×8 grayscale grid.
  3. Pixel values are mapped to the dataset's 0–16 scale, then divided by 16, matching training.
  4. The ONNX model runs in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly). Nothing is sent to a server.

The training images are tiny and centered, so clean, single-stroke digits that fill the pad work best.

Trained in PyTorch on scikit-learn's digits dataset, exported to ONNX, served as static files.
